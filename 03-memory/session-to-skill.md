# Prompts: drafting a skill

Two ways to get a first draft. Either way, the group cuts and edits it.

## Session to skill

Run this in the same chat as the ticket you just worked:

> Turn what we just did, including my corrections, into a reusable skill: a SKILL.md with a trigger, numbered steps and a "Done when".

## Interview me

Useful when the procedure lives in someone's head rather than in a session:

> Ask me questions, one at a time, about how we do <task> here. Then draft a SKILL.md with a trigger, numbered steps and a "Done when".

## Then fix the trigger

The `description` is the trigger: it's the only part the agent sees until it picks the skill. Write it in two halves: what the skill does, then when to use it.

> Review a branch or pull request against its ticket and the team conventions in AGENTS.md, and report blocking and non-blocking findings. Use when the user asks to review a PR, review a branch, or check work before opening a PR.

Ask: when should it fire, and when shouldn't it?
